### Turnover

**Turnover** turns a Final Cut Pro XML export into an AAF with embedded audio that opens straight in Pro Tools. It was built by a Korean TV drama director who got tired of painful turnovers to the mix stage.

- Clip positions, timecode, clip volume and fades travel into the session.
- Tracks are organised by Final Cut Pro audio role.
- Markers come across too.
- Before export, Turnover checks that every source file is present, so missing media is caught before the session reaches the mixer.
- Handles are adjustable, set in frames.

Tested against Pro Tools. Universal app for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 15 or later, available on the Mac App Store.

Free download with every feature on, output limited to the first five minutes. Full Unlock is a one-time in-app purchase of US$49.99, with no subscription.
